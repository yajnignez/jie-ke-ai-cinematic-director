# Quality Rubric

Score silently. Rewrite any result below 80/100 or failing a critical item.

## Critical checks

- One primary narrative change fits the duration.
- Subject, action, space, and ending state are visible.
- Camera movement has one motivation and an end composition.
- Light has a source and a coherent direction.
- Focal length and aperture are explicit when camera optics affect the shot.
- Actor prompts contain objective, obstacle, causal behavior, and a turn.
- Image-to-video prompts do not unnecessarily redesign the source image.
- Identity, eyeline, wardrobe, key light, and physical continuity are protected.
- No unauthorized real-person likeness or voice is required.

## Scored criteria

| Criterion | Points | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Dramatic purpose | 15 | visible change and viewer effect |
| Playable action | 15 | physical, directional, bounded action |
| Beat progression | 15 | trigger, escalation, turn, settle |
| Camera and optics | 15 | framing, movement, focal length, aperture, focus |
| Light and material | 10 | motivated source, color, texture |
| Continuity | 10 | stable identity, space, light, sound |
| Generation robustness | 10 | concise constraints and no conflicts |
| Iterability | 10 | one-variable next change |

## Common rejection reasons

Adjective-heavy prompts, multiple unrelated actions, dramatic camera movement without story motivation, repeated description of image-fixed content, shallow depth of field that loses the eyes, instant emotional changes, unsupported platform controls, generic negative prompts, and endings that cannot hold for an edit.
