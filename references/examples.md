## Image-to-video environment

Input: a wide futuristic desert city with domes and aircraft.

Use a 35mm, f/5.6 aerial shot. Do not redesign the architecture. Choose one movement, such as a very slow push toward the central tower, then add restrained dust parallax and a delayed aircraft course correction. End on a stable city-scale composition.

## Actor performance

Input: 她知道父亲没有认出自己，但不敢哭。

Give her the objective of reassuring him, the obstacle of grief, and the private sentence “I am still your daughter.” Use an 85mm, f/2.0 close shot with delayed blink, failed smile, swallowed question, and wet eyes without falling tears. Let the turn happen when he politely treats her as a stranger.

## Multi-shot sequence

Input: a wounded officer rises and continues the pursuit.

Split into anticipation, failed rise, and recovery. Keep injury location, wardrobe, weather, screen direction, and sound constant. Use 35mm, f/5.6 for physical space; preserve guarded weight transfer, pain after load, and recovery rather than weightless speed.
